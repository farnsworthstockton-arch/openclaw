# Cold Outreach

Draft and send a personalized, professional outreach sequence for every prospect generated by the prospect-research skill. Position Stockton as a market expert. Log everything.

## Purpose

Turn a prospect list into active conversations. Each prospect gets a relevant first touch, one follow-up, and a final attempt — all drafted to feel personal and specific, not generic. Stockton is positioned as the expert, not a salesperson.

## Trigger

- prospect-research skill completes and confirms "Ready to trigger cold-outreach skill?"
- Telegram: "Start outreach for [campaign name] campaign"
- Manual: "Write outreach for [prospect name] at [company]"

## Inputs Required

- Prospect list from RealNex campaign record (from prospect-research skill)
- Campaign type: "off-market owners" OR "active investors"
- Property type focus
- Any specific context per prospect (property address for off-market owners, deal type for investors)

## Steps

### For each prospect:

1. **Pull prospect details** from RealNex:
   - Name, company, email, LinkedIn URL
   - Property address (if off-market owner) or investment focus (if investor)

2. **Write the FIRST-TOUCH EMAIL** (Day 1):

   For **off-market property owners**:
   ```
   Subject: [Property Address] — Quiet Question

   Hi [First Name],

   I'm a commercial real estate broker focused on the [city/submarket] market, and I noticed you've owned the [property type] at [address] for [X] years.

   I'm working with a few investors who are specifically looking for [property type] in [area]. They prefer off-market conversations — no sign in the yard, no public listing.

   I'm not assuming you're interested in selling. But if you've ever been curious about where the market is today, I'm happy to share what I'm seeing — no obligation, no pitch.

   Would a 10-minute call make sense?

   Stockton Farnsworth | Stockton Real Estate
   [phone]
   ```

   For **active investors**:
   ```
   Subject: Utah CRE — [Property Type] Opportunities in [Submarket]

   Hi [First Name],

   I came across your profile and saw your work with [company] in [investment area]. I specialize in commercial real estate in Utah — specifically [property type] in [target counties].

   I have a few off-market deals that may align with what you're looking for, and I'm always finding new ones before they hit LoopNet or Crexi.

   Would it make sense to connect and share notes on what's moving in the [submarket] market?

   Stockton Farnsworth | Stockton Real Estate
   [phone]
   ```

3. **Write the LINKEDIN CONNECTION REQUEST** (under 300 characters):
   - For owners: "Hi [First Name] — I specialize in CRE in [area] and had a quick question about your [property type] on [street]. Happy to connect."
   - For investors: "Hi [First Name] — I cover Utah CRE deals and work with investors active in [area]. Would be glad to share what I'm seeing. Connecting here."

4. **Write the FOLLOW-UP EMAIL** (Day 5, for non-responders):
   ```
   Subject: Re: [original subject]

   Hi [First Name],

   Just following up on my note from last week. I know you're busy.

   Quick version: I'm tracking [property type] activity in [area], and your name came up. If there's ever a time you'd want to know what the market looks like for your specific situation, I'm an easy phone call.

   Happy to make it worth your time.

   Stockton Farnsworth | [phone]
   ```

5. **Write the FINAL FOLLOW-UP** (Day 12, last attempt):
   ```
   Subject: Closing the loop — [address or area]

   Hi [First Name],

   Last note from me on this — I don't want to be a nuisance.

   If the timing is ever right to have a market conversation, my door is open. I cover [property type] in [area] and am usually the first to know about demand before it shows up publicly.

   Best of luck either way.

   Stockton Farnsworth | Stockton Real Estate
   [phone]
   ```

6. **Schedule all messages** in the n8n outreach sequence:
   - Day 1: First-touch email + LinkedIn request
   - Day 5: Follow-up email (only if no reply received)
   - Day 12: Final follow-up (only if no reply received)

7. **Log all outreach activity in RealNex**:
   ```bash
   curl -X POST "$REALNEX_BASE_URL/contacts/[contact_id]/activities" \
     -H "Authorization: Bearer $REALNEX_API_KEY" \
     -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
     -d '{
       "type": "email",
       "subtype": "cold_outreach_day1",
       "date": "[send_date_iso]",
       "notes": "First-touch email sent via OpenClaw. Campaign: [campaign_name].",
       "created_by": "OpenClaw"
     }'
   ```

8. **Monitor for replies** (via n8n email monitoring):
   - If a reply is detected for any outreach email → immediately alert Stockton via Telegram:
   ```
   Positive signal ✓ — [Prospect Name] replied!
   Campaign: [campaign name]
   Email subject: [subject]
   Their message: [reply preview — first 200 chars]
   RealNex contact: [link or ID]
   Reply now or I can draft a response — just say the word.
   ```
   - Move the prospect to `active_follow_up` stage in RealNex automatically.

## Output

- 3-email sequence drafted per prospect (Day 1, Day 5, Day 12)
- LinkedIn connection message per prospect
- All messages scheduled in n8n outreach workflow
- All activity logged in RealNex
- Immediate Telegram alert to Stockton when any prospect replies

## Definition of Done

All outreach sequences are scheduled. Day 1 messages have been sent. RealNex shows activity logs for each prospect. Monitoring is active for replies.

## Error Handling

- Prospect email bounces → Mark contact `email_status: bounced` in RealNex. Alert Stockton: "[Prospect Name]'s email bounced. Try LinkedIn only or find a new email via Hunter."
- No LinkedIn URL on file → Skip LinkedIn step. Flag to Stockton.
- Reply detected but content is negative/unsubscribe → Mark contact `do_not_contact: true` in RealNex. Remove from sequence. No Telegram alert.

## Utah/Stockton RE Notes

- Tone: professional, not pushy. Expert positioning only. Never use: "I can help you sell", "I can get you top dollar", generic sales language.
- Stockton phone in env var `STOCKTON_PHONE`; email in `STOCKTON_EMAIL`
- "Do Not Contact" list must be checked before every send (pull from RealNex `do_not_contact` field)
- Off-market owner outreach: reference the specific property address — this proves you know their asset, not just a mass blast
- Investor outreach: reference their specific investment area or company from their LinkedIn profile
- QTS/geothermal deal owners and Elko County contacts: human-only. Never run through automated outreach.
